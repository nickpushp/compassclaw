01 How much does it cost to hire movers in California?

The cost depends on several factors, such as the size of your move, the distance, whether you need packing services, and any special handling requirements. Local moves within the same city typically start at $400, while long-distance moves across California or out of state can range anywhere from $3,000–$10,000+.

We provide detailed, transparent estimates upfront so you know exactly what to expect. Contact us by phone, online, or on-site to build a custom move plan and get an estimate.

02 Do you offer packing services, or do I need to pack myself?

We offer both full-service and partial packing options. If you want us to handle everything, our crew will bring all the supplies and pack your entire home. If you prefer to pack some belongings yourself and leave the rest to us, such as fragile dishes or bulky furniture, we’re happy to accommodate that approach. If you prefer to pack every box on your own, that option is available as well.

Packing services are optional and priced separately, so you only pay for what you need.

03 How far in advance should I book my move?

We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance, especially during peak moving season (May through September). That said, we handle last-minute moves whenever possible—sometimes within just a few days if we have availability.

The sooner you book, the more flexibility you’ll have with scheduling your preferred moving date.

04 Are my belongings insured during the move?

Yes. We’re fully licensed, bonded, and insured. Your belongings are covered under basic valuation protection at no additional cost, which provides $0.60 per pound per item. Full value additional insurance is also available for the entire move or specific items only, as well as property insurance.

05 Do you move items like pianos, safes, or antiques?

Yes, as a part of a move. We specialize in moving heavy, oversized, and high-value items that require extra care and expertise. Our crews use professional equipment—furniture dollies, straps, ramps, and protective padding—to move pianos, safes, antiques, and other specialty items safely. Please make sure to notify your Moving Consultant about Special Handling Items you need to be moved, so the appropriate crew and tools are sent.

06 What areas do you serve?

We operate out of three offices across California: North Hollywood (serving LA County and nearby counties – Ventura, Orange, San Bernardino, Riverside), Poway (serving San Diego County and adjacent parts of Orange and Riverside Counties), and Santa Clara (serving the Bay Area and its wider surroundings – San Mateo, Alameda, San Francisco, Contra Costa). We handle local moves, as well as long-distance moves across California and to other states.

If you’re unsure if we cover your area, call us, and we’ll let you know.

07 What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Discover). We also offer flexible financing plans for customers who prefer to spread payments over time. Ask about financing options during your consultation.
